## Full description
Mom has left, and a tiny café is all you have. The shelves are dusty, the hall is empty, but the first cat is already waiting at the window for an espresso. Open the doors of Warm Paw: you stand behind the counter, and your guests and helpers are cats!

🐾 A COZY CAT CAFÉ SIMULATOR
Build every order on your tray: coffee, tea, cocoa, milk, syrup, ice, cinnamon and cream. Add toppings, serve while it's hot. Syrniki, éclairs, croissants and honey cake fill the sweet corner of the display.

☕ 27 DRINKS AND PASTRIES
Discover recipes as you go. A recipe cheat sheet is always one tap away, and a wrong tray can be thrown into the bin and rebuilt.

😺 CATS WITH CHARACTER
Every cat has a personality and a talent: Gingerbread boosts tips, Cloudy waits patiently, Ginger loves pastries, Bun keeps your cup warm. Meet them, treat them, befriend them. Some mysterious guests will only order a drink described in a riddle.

🛠 RENOVATE AND DECORATE
Stars you earn in shifts turn into renovations: the sign, the menu, the terrace, the second floor. Place tables, plants, poufs and rugs wherever you like. The cozier the café, the bigger the tip bonus. Everything is drawn in warm top-down pixel art.

📖 A STORY ABOUT MOM'S CAFÉ
Six chapters of letters, cat friends and surprises. Find out where Mom went and who the secret guest the cats whisper about is.

🌦 A LIVING TOWN
Morning, evening, rain and snow: the town outside changes, and so does the mood of every shift. Daily tasks, streak rewards and seasonal events keep it fresh.

FEATURES
• One-handed play, a shift takes 2–3 minutes
• Fully offline
• No forced ads: watching a video is always your choice
• English and Russian
• Small download and simple graphics
• Family friendly: no violence, nothing scary

Fans of café games, restaurant sims, cats, relaxing casual games and pixel art will feel at home in Warm Paw.
